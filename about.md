## Bio
Pierre is a PhD student at IMT Atlantique advised by Alexandre Reiffers-Masson and Françoise Sailhan. Before his PhD, Pierre worked for a year in a consulting firm in Access management.
Pierre graduated in 2022 from IMT Atlantique as a french engineer with a specialization in Machine Learning and Cybersecurity.  Previously he was in Preparatory classes for French Engineering Schools in Nantes.

## Research interests: 

Pierre is interested in the security, modeling, orchestration, and performance of the Cloud. In particular, he is interested in using mathematical models, such as Markov decision processes or queues, to analyze cloud systems and derive performance metrics. These models serve as a basis to develop algorithms that improve the security and the performance of the Cloud. 

## Keywords:

Cloud, Cybersecurity, Markov decision process
