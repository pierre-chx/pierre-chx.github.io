## Bio
Pierre is a PhD student at IMT Atlantique advised by Associate Professor Alexandre
Reiffers-Masson and Professor Françoise Sailhan. Pierre graduated in 2022 from the engineering school IMT Atlantique with a specialization in Machine Learning and Cybersecurity. After graduating, he worked for a year in a consulting firm in Access management before starting a PhD. Previously he was in Preparatory classes for French Engineering Schools in Nantes.

## Research interests: 
Cybersecurity, Moving Target Defense, Applied Mathematics, Game Theory, Machine Learning, Reinforcement Learning

## Thesis: 
Moving Target Defense to improve the security of virtualized networks
